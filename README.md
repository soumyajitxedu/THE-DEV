## 🌱 What is this?

This is my **progress tracker**. Not a portfolio, not a library — just a pile of experiments, half-built models, school assignments, embedded tinkering, and pure vibecoded chaos thrown into one repo so I can look back and see how far I've come.

Some of it works. Most of it doesn't. That's the point. 🫠

Most of these files I don't even remember writing. Some of them I don't understand anymore. A few I refuse to delete out of pure sentimental attachment. This repo is a **time capsule**, not a showcase.

---

## 🧠 A little context

I'm 17. I'm preparing for board exams. I'm writing this the night before my geography exam, probably staying up till 1 or 2 AM, definitely regretting it tomorrow.

This repo is where my **AI obsession** started. When I got into this sector, I learned a lot — logic, core concepts, PyTorch, the lore, the math behind the magic. I wanted to be **Hinton** someday. Or maybe that retired researcher everyone reads about in textbooks. Maybe I still do. Maybe I don't.

Right now the commits are blank. Nothing new in the last few months. Board exam pressure, life, burnout — whatever you want to call it. I'm not quitting. I'm just **paused**.

When I come back, I'll keep pushing. Until then, this trail stays here — proof that I was here, that I built things, that I cared.

---

## 🧩 The Karpathy era

At one point I got **obsessed with Andrej Karpathy**. I wanted to do what he did — build from scratch, understand everything, teach it back. I tried to copy him the right way.

I failed. **Not once. Not twice. Four times.**

I watched the video. I took notes. I did shitty experiments. I tried to rewrite it from memory without looking — especially **"Let's build GPT"** and **"Let's build the Transformer."** Every time I got stuck. Every time I went back to the video. Every time I understood a little more than the last attempt.

Along the way, **Claude and Gemini helped a lot**. Then I **shifted to DeepSeek** and it stuck. Between them and Karpathy, I learned enough AI to actually follow what the big names are saying. When **Ilya** or **Demis** talk fundamentals, I get it. I can follow the reasoning. That's not nothing.

I'm not at their level. I never claimed to be. But I can **listen** now, and that took work.

## ⚡ The field moves too fast

In the last **6 months** alone: RAG, MCP, agents, function calling, tool use, vector DBs, small models, on-device inference — **so much dropped so quickly**.

There was a time I was **obsessed with the lore**. Every paper, every release, every drama in the AI world. Now? I don't pick them up anymore. The obsession cooled. Not because I stopped caring — because life got loud.

Maybe after boards I'll come back to it. Try to be my **prime** again. Make the contribution graph green again.

I have a lot of goals. Let's see what happens.

## 📈 Why public?

Because **progress should be visible** — even the messy kind.  
Anyone can show off finished work. Fewer people show the drafts, the crashes, the rewrites, the "what was I thinking" commits.

This repo is the **in-between**. The middle. The part most people hide.

I don't. 🌚
